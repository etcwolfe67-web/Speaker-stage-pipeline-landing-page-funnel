BEFORE WE TALK

GOOD QUESTIONS
STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Is this a coaching program?

No The core offer is done-for-you execution and agency representation We develop the assets and perform the outreach rather than simply teaching you how to do it

Do you guarantee a certain number of bookings?

No fixed number of bookings is promised here We commit to a defined operating scope, including at least 150 pitches per month, while selection and booking decisions remain with event organizers

What if I'm already an established speaker?

That is precisely the kind of expertise this service is designed to support We assess your positioning, existing assets, commercial offers, and the opportunities that best fit your goals

Are all speaking opportunities paid?

No Some engagements pay fees; others can create exposure, relationships, and downstream consulting opportunities We evaluate their commercial value rather than treating every stage as equal

What happens after a speaking engagement?

Where appropriate, we use audience capture, automated follow-up, and ongoing marketing to develop the new relationships created through the event

Why do you charge commissions?

Our compensation includes commissions on specified speaking fees and eligible downstream business so we participate in the commercial success we're working to create The precise terms are set out in the agreement
